# TRACTION — Go-To-Market Plan & Revenue Projections

## Product
**Traction** — AI-powered sales outreach platform for local service businesses.
Automates lead finding, personalized outreach, follow-ups, and meeting booking.

## Target Market
- Local service businesses (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, dental, legal, real estate)
- 5-50 employees
- Monthly marketing budget: $500-$5,000
- Pain point: Cold calling doesn't work, can't afford enterprise sales tools

## Pricing
| Plan | Price | Target Customer |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Starter | $49/mo | Solo operators |
| Pro | $99/mo | Growing businesses |
| Agency | $249/mo | Agencies/multi-location |

## Revenue Projections

- **Customers:** 100 Pro ($99) + 20 Agency ($249) = ~$10,380
- **GTM:** Launch on Product Hunt, post in 20+ local business FB groups, $2k Facebook Ads
- **Key metric:** 5% conversion from free trial

### Month 2: $25,000 — EARLY TRACTION
- **Customers:** 200 Pro + 40 Agency + 50 Starter = ~$25,000

### Month 3: $55,000 — SCALE
- **Customers:** 400 Pro + 80 Agency + 150 Starter = ~$55,000

- **Key metric:** CAC < $150, LTV > $600

## Unit Economics

| Gross Margin | 75%+ |
| Monthly Churn | < 5% |

## Channels (Ranked by ROI)
1. **Facebook Groups** — local business owners (~$0 cost, high conversion)
2. **Product Hunt Launch** — viral launch, press coverage
3. **Google Ads** — "AI sales for [industry]" keywords
4. **Referral Program** — incentivized word-of-mouth
5. **Cold Email** — to agencies and franchise operators
6. **Partnerships** — CRMs, industry associations
7. **Content Marketing** — "How to automate sales" guides
8. **LinkedIn Ads** — targeting business owners

## Key Risks
- AI personalization quality not meeting expectations
- Higher churn than projected in early months
- Competition from incumbents (Close, Outreach, Apollo)
- Slower enterprise adoption than modeled
- Regulatory changes around AI/automated outreach
